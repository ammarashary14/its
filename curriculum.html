The wonder in everyday things

Choose an everyday object. Look closely, draw what you notice, and share one question it makes you wonder about.

  1. Pick a familiar object, such as a leaf or pencil.
  2. Look closely and draw three things you notice.
  3. Share a question with a partner or the group.
