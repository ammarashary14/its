WHAT WE’RE BUILDING

More room
to understand.

Breaking the Sky connects volunteers with schools to teach from a curriculum adapted to different grade levels. We also invite students and families to reach out for individual support.

Our focus is math, biology, chemistry, physics, and earth science—with space to ask questions and work through an idea together.
